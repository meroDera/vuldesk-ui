## Problem Statement

The live site (www.vuldesk.com, commit `6793c65`; on 2026-10-30 all 34 built files were
byte-identical to the live files) presents Vuldesk as one person:

- `src/pages/index.astro:34` "I build AI tools that read your records and give you answers."
- `src/pages/index.astro:105` "I am Kushal. I run Vuldesk from Kathmandu, and I build every tool myself."
- `src/pages/index.astro:112` the Q&A "What if you disappear?"
- `index.astro:37` "I build a tool", `:42` "one of my tools", `:46` "Tell me about your pile",
  `:58` "I built a booking system", `:85` "I watch it and fix it. I reply", `:92` "The price I
  quote", `:121-122` "under your account, not mine. I never use them", `:134` "I overlap",
  `:144` "Tell me about it ... I will reply", `:148` and `SiteHeader.astro:13` "Email me"
- `src/navigation.js:15` the email draft "Hi Kushal,", `src/config.yaml:14` the meta and og
  description "I build a tool ...", the og card image text, and `src/pages/404.astro:13`
  "I could not find that page."

The owner, verbatim: "currently its showing as single person company, which is not true." The
August design chose the "I" voice on purpose for a solo operator (August office-hours D9). The company now has a
team: several people build the tools (owner, Q4).

## Demand Evidence

- No buyer has said that the one-person story cost Vuldesk interest. The owner could not name
  one (Q3: "i cant name the customer"). The owner's hypothesis, verbatim: "single person company
  is lacking advantage of having a team factor. potential customers are not interested because
  its showing off as single person company".
- The truth fix needs no demand evidence: the live page states something false.
- Proxy evidence only. In the cold-read test of wireframe v4, 3 of 3 personas read "a team"
  (guesses 3-10, 3-8 and 3-6 people). Personas are model stand-ins, not buyers. Raw file:
  `~/.gstack/projects/meroDera-vuldesk-ui/designs/wireframe-20261030/cold-read-v4.json`.
- That team read did not survive D10. In wireframe v4.1 (no products row, and no city in the
  team line), 0 of 6 runs read "a team": all answered "not sure", with guesses of 1-5 and 1-3.
  The reason given: "Only Kushal gets a name and a photo, and nobody else on 'our team' is ever
  shown, so it could be just him." On the phone: "maybe one guy saying 'we'". "We" and an
  unproven "our team" do not carry the story, so the hero now states the size (D12).
- The control run confirms the problem with the same harness: 3 of 3 personas read the live
  page as one person, and each guessed 1. Their words: "Some guy builds AI computer programs",
  "One guy builds AI software", "A one-person shop (it says 'I')".
- Market scan of 34 small service-company sites, October 2026
  (`docs/designs/assets/market-scan-2026-10.md`): 31 of 34 write "we", 0 of 34 write "I"
  throughout, 24 of 34 name at least one person, and the 6 that state a headcount sell the
  small number ("Six people who pick up the phone"). Buyer research in the same file: users
  fear that a small business is less reliable (NN/g); photos of real staff raise trust;
  published prices rank first among website elements (Clutch 2020, TrustRadius 2022).
- A persona's first question (not a buyer): "Can you do that foreclosure-case thing on my
  county lists ...?" (home-buying-president persona).

## Status Quo

Buyers meet a one-page letter from one person. The August built page passed comprehension
(8/9/9), so the problem is not comprehension. It is a false company claim, and a page that
reads as a single point of failure.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

Unchanged from the August doc: a US business owner or operator with no programmers, buying a
done-for-you AI tool at published fixed prices. The wedge of this change is the smallest edit
that makes every company claim true and reads as a team: copy changes on the existing page, no
new pages. The booking link waits for the owner's URL (P5).

## Constraints

- Only owner-confirmed facts reach the page (P1).
  - Confirmed: several people build the tools (Q4); the team overlaps US business hours every
    day (Q6); the team replies to every buyer within one business day (Q6); 4 people work at
    Vuldesk today, 3 build the tools and 1 handles clients (Q7); Vuldesk sets up everything in
    the client's name, and the AI company bills the client directly, usually $20 to $100 a
    month for a small tool (Q8; the first answer matched the example text word for word, so the
    owner was asked again and confirmed the figure: "correct").
  - Confirmed for the example and the Q&A: Vuldesk built a tool that helped real estate
    agents filter out properties, with AI making the judgements (Q10); the client's own staff
    check the items that the tool is not sure about (Q11).
  - True but not stated on the page: 2 of the 4 people are part-time (Q7). The owner chose to
    leave this out (D13: "a team of four" is true).
  - Not true or not confirmed, so not on the page: that the whole team works in Kathmandu (Q6:
    not selected); that a second person can look after each client's tool (D7: not true
    today); team names and team photos (Q1: skipped); who answers buyer email.
- CHECK 5 in `scripts/check-integrity.mjs`: at most 450 visible words and zero terms from
  `docs/designs/assets/jargon-inventory.md`, including meta strings, alt text and decoded
  mailto drafts.
- Static Astro 4 site on GitHub Pages. Every push to `main` deploys after the `verify` job
  (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). No form backend.
- `DESIGN.md` set-letter system: light only, no boxes, five blocks, one primary action.
- Timebox (August P5, kept by D25): the build ships within two working days of its start, and
  outbound outranks site work. P6 must pass first: if P6 has not passed at the end of the second
  working day, the owner decides to ship or to hold.

  Q10-Q12. (The agreed text said care promises carry the team advantage, with facts from Q4 and
  Q6 only. The owner's D12 choice, "State the team size near the top", revised it.)
- **P5 (revised).** Scope: the voice change, the team line, the trust answers (D11, Q8, Q11,
  Q14), and a booking link beside the email button (the adopted outside-view spec)
  once the owner gives the booking URL. The layout,
  prices, proof and look stay. No team grid and no new pages. (The agreed text said: copy
  change only; layout, prices, proof and look stay. The own-products row that E added was

- **P6 (revised by D14 and D16).** Proof before ship: `npm test` passes on the built page, and
  the ship gate passes. Ship gate: run the five-second read (the harness's three personas, one
  standalone prompt) 3 times per persona on each screen, the desktop first screen (1280 x 720)
  and the phone first screen (375 x 667), on the new build and on a build of `6793c65`: 36
  five-second runs.
  Pass when, for every persona and screen, the new build's median confidence is not lower than
  the `6793c65` median, and all 18 runs on the new build answer "a team" to "how many people
  work here?". The gate also runs the harness's whole-page read (its two-step prompt) once per
  persona on each build: 6 runs. That part passes when the new build's would-contact count is
  not lower than the `6793c65` count, and every phrase that this change adds or rewrites and
  that loses 3 of 3 personas on the new build has a recorded owner decision before ship (D18
  covers the cost line and the proof line). Unchanged lines that lose 3 of 3, such as "Reads
  one kind of record.", stay open questions for a later copy round (D19, owner, 2026-10-31,
  after review finding R3-1). Quotes in lost_at count as one phrase when they come from the same
  sentence of the page. The gate runs on the PR head commit; a later commit that changes visible
  text, meta strings, the og card or the hero CSS voids the result (eng review E1). When a part
  fails, that part runs once more on the same build, with fresh subagents and the same prompts; a
  second fail goes to the owner, who decides to ship or to rework, recorded like D16 (D22).
  (The agreed bar was 3 of 3 at confidence 7 or more and 0 of 3 "one person". The
  live page itself fails that bar in this harness, because the clinic-owner persona gives 6 to
  every version. D14 made the bar relative to the live page; D16 showed that a single run moves
  by about one point, so the gate compares medians.)
