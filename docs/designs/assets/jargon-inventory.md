## Missed items added by completeness pass

- 'discount it accordingly' (index.astro:48) — 'discount' here means 'give this evidence less weight', but on a page that sells at a fixed price the money sense is primed; a scanning reader can misread the proof section's honesty disclaimer as price talk.
- 'Product:' field in the pre-filled contact email (navigation.js:11) — the auditors flagged the 'Stack:' blank but missed that the first blank, 'Product:', fails the same way: a clinic owner with a wish but no software product meets a form that presumes one, two lines before 'Stack:' finishes the job.
- index.astro:48 — "This one is my own project, not a client engagement, and you should discount it accordingly." 'Discount' is figurative here (give the evidence less weight). The target reader knows 'discount' only as a price cut, so the proof section opens with what reads as a pricing statement.
- index.astro:48 — "What it demonstrates is the mechanics, and the mechanics are the part that transfers." 'Mechanics' is abstract; to this reader, mechanics fix cars. The sentence that justifies the whole foreclosure example rests on two abstractions, and the auditors flagged only 'transfers'.
- index.astro:58 — heading "Reasons across multiple calls". Bare 'calls' reads as phone calls, and 'reasons' as a verb applied to software is AI vocabulary. The heading confirms the agent-is-a-person misreading before the flagged 'tool calls' even appears.
- index.astro:60 — "the agent makes several tool calls and thinks between them". 'Thinks' gives the software a mind. Because the page never says the agent is software, this line actively supports the person reading.
- index.astro:35 — "The county publishes the list." Assumes a US county-government context and prior knowledge that counties publish foreclosure lists. 'The list' has no antecedent; a cold reader cannot picture what list or where.
- index.astro:30 — "An agent read 118 foreclosure filings." 'Filings' is court-record vocabulary. The auditors flagged 'dead', 'going to sale', and 'cured', but not 'filings' itself, which sits in the largest text on the page next to 'agent'.
- index.astro:137 and :274 — "Gamma IVF, still running" / "Gamma IVF is public and you can open it right now." An unexplained proper noun. The page never says Gamma IVF is a fertility clinic whose website he built; the reader meets a brand name plus an acronym with no introduction.
- index.astro:144 — "Twenty months later it still answers." 'Answers' in the web-server sense (responds to requests). To a lay reader a website does not 'answer'; the availability proof is stated in an idiom.
- index.astro:144 — "the system stays up." Uptime idiom; 'up' is spatial to a lay reader. The exact thing being proven is invisible.
